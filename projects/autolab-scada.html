What I built

AutoLab SCADA brings temperature monitoring, manual motor and fan commands, timed process recipes and experiment records into one browser interface. It was built as a small-scale laboratory automation system rather than a full industrial SCADA installation.

How it works

  • Dashboard: Temperature readings, status indicators, trends and a timestamped activity log.
  • Process recipes: Configurable steps for motor and fan operation, with duration and repeat settings.
  • Experiment records: Previous runs and history with export options.
  • Hardware integration: A separate FastAPI/PySerial gateway exchanges commands and telemetry with Arduino firmware. Local SQLite storage records hardware-side data.

Demo and hardware modes

Anyone can open the public browser demo and test its interface without hardware. Demo measurements and actuator states are simulated. The private operating mode requires an authorized local gateway and connected Arduino; it is not available simply by opening the public link.

This is an educational prototype, not a certified industrial control or safety system.
