Intelligent battery thermal management

A third-year prototype using two temperature sensors and an Arduino to monitor a battery setup and respond to rising temperature.

System overview

The prototype combines a DS18B20 contact sensor and an MLX90614 infrared sensor with an Arduino Uno. It evaluates temperature readings, monitors sensor faults and controls a buzzer, cooling fan and motor load. A 16×2 LCD shows the current status.

Protection logic

01 / WARNING32°C

Audible temperature warning.

02 / COOLING35°C

Relay-controlled fan is switched on.

03 / LOAD CUTOFF40°C

The motor load is disconnected.

Hysteresis reduces repeated switching around the thresholds. The firmware can continue using one valid sensor when the other fails.

Prototype observations

The documented test recorded fan activation at 35.24°C, load disconnection at 40.41°C and recovery at 31.74°C. These observations illustrate the implemented control sequence.

This educational prototype is not a substitute for a battery management system (BMS) or independently validated battery safety equipment.
